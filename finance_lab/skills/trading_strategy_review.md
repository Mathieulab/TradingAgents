# Trading Strategy Review Skill

Use for reviewing a proposed strategy before backtesting or paper trading.

Checklist:

- define entry and exit rules,
- define position sizing,
- define stop loss and take profit logic,
- identify data requirements,
- identify lookahead-bias risks,
- define fees and slippage assumptions,
- define metrics needed to judge success.

Reject strategies that only optimize win rate without expectancy and drawdown checks.
