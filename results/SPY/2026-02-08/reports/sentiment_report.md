4. **Sector-Specific Impacts**
   - **Retail and Consumer Goods**: Articles on retail stocks (BJ’s, Kroger) and Walmart’s valuation shifts indicate macroeconomic pressures, which indirectly affect SPY’s performance via consumer spending trends.
   - **Tech and Innovation**: The S&P 500’s potential to hit 8,000 is tied to tech sector performance, as tech companies dominate the index’s weight.
